Basis and limits

Not legal advice. Mapping findings to legal provisions is orientation, not a statement of conformity and not audit evidence. Where applicability depends on a classification that could not be settled here, this report names it as a question for legal rather than as a finding.

The document was assessed, not the practice. A policy nobody follows is rated well here. Coverage figures are ratios over this catalogue of checks, not over the full text of any document, and there is no overall score in per cent — a single blended number would hide the divergence that matters.
